// About

Every sport is a game of forces.

Game of Forces explains the physics behind sport in plain language. Why a car sticks to the track at 300 km/h, why a ball swings, why a sprinter leans: it all comes down to forces, and none of it needs a textbook to understand.

The series

LAPTHESIS is the Formula 1 series. Before every Grand Prix it breaks down the technical upgrades each team brings: what changed on the car, why, and what to look for once the cars are on track.

The Winning Formula is the newsletter, where sports meet science. Each issue explains the science behind a different game, made simple and fun for students. Issue 1 covered swing, seam and spin in cricket, and the geometry of angles in badminton.
